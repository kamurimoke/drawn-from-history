Drawn From History

Real decisions, drawn badly, explained properly.

Every episode is one person, one impossible position, and the single decision that turned it — told in ten minutes with stick figures, because the lesson does not need a budget.

What the channel is

Short documentaries about moments where someone's plan died and they had to decide what the job was now. The history is researched and sourced; the drawings are deliberately plain. Every description lists what the episode was built from, and corrections go in a pinned comment.

Watch at youtube.com/@drawnfromhistory2026.

How the episodes are made

Script and research first, then narration, then one drawing per beat, then assembly — all produced on hardware we own. Illustrations and narration are generated with local models and directed by hand; the historical claims are checked against the sources named in each description, not generated.
