Drawn From History

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers two things: this website, and the private tool ("Drawn From History Uploader") that publishes episodes to the Drawn From History YouTube channel.

Who operates them

Both are operated by the owner of the Drawn From History channel, as a personal project. There is no company behind them and no staff other than the owner.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts and has no forms. Nothing about a visit is collected or stored by us. The pages are served by our hosting provider, which keeps its own ordinary server logs; we do not read or export them.

The uploader

The uploader is used by the channel owner on the owner's own machine, to publish the owner's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users, so it never touches another person's account or data.

It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to the data Google handles.

What it accesses, and why

  • Upload a video — to publish an episode and set its title, description, tags, thumbnail and caption track.
  • Read the channel — to confirm the upload went to the right channel and to read back an episode's status.
  • Channel analytics — to read view and watch-time figures for the owner's own channel, to decide what to make next.

It does not read, collect or store anything about viewers, commenters or subscribers, and it does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision about a person.

What is stored, where, and for how long

One authorisation token for the owner's own Google account, held as a file on the owner's private machine with owner-only permissions, outside any shared or published location. It is never transmitted anywhere except to Google when refreshing access, is never sold, shared or given to a third party, and is deleted when access is revoked or when it is no longer needed. Video files, scripts and drawings are the channel's own work and are kept on the same machine.

Revoking access, and deletion

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops the uploader from reaching the account. When access is revoked, the stored token is deleted. Anyone may also write to the address below to ask what is held and to have it deleted.

Children

Neither the site nor the uploader is directed at children, and neither knowingly collects information from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Write to us through the channel: youtube.com/@drawnfromhistory2026, or leave a comment on any episode.
